================================================================================
CONSUMER SENTIMENT INTELLIGENCE HUB — FEATURE IDEAS FROM COMPETITIVE RESEARCH
================================================================================
Source: deep-research competitive scan (Dovetail, Condens, Conveo, NotebookLM,
Glean, Quantilope inColor, Discuss.io, Remesh, + peer-reviewed AI-rigour studies).
All findings verified 3-0. Date: 2026-07-17.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
VALIDATION (already best-in-class — keep and market it)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Citation-grounded "Ask the Archive" with sentence-level citations and
  machine-verified verbatim quotes is the SAME paradigm as Dovetail, NotebookLM
  and Glean. We are at parity on the hardest part.
- Research rigour is a genuine market differentiator, not just an internal
  constraint: 47-69% of AI-generated citations are hallucinated/inaccurate in
  peer-reviewed studies (one lit review: ~66%). Our machine-verified quotes,
  cautious/small-base language, PII redaction and source traceability directly
  answer this. Every NEW AI feature below must inherit these guardrails.

================================================================================
QUICK WINS (low effort, fit current architecture, preserve rigour)
================================================================================

1. AI AUTO-TAG SUGGESTIONS INTO THE HUMAN-GATED TAXONOMY
   - What: extend the existing Haiku ingest pass to propose theme tags per
     chunk; reviewer confirms/rejects at the review gate (suggestions, not live).
   - Exemplars: Dovetail "Magic Highlight", Condens AI tagging suggestions.
   - Complexity: LOW. We already run a suggestion pass; this widens it.
   - Rigour: preserved (human confirms).

2. HIGHLIGHT / VERBATIM-LEVEL SENTIMENT FLAGS (OPTIONAL, CAVEATED OVERLAY)
   - What: positive / negative / neutral flag per quote or highlight, shown as
     an optional overlay with the usual caveats (no false statistical precision).
   - Exemplars: Dovetail, Discuss.io, Quantilope inColor.
   - Complexity: LOW-MEDIUM.
   - Rigour: must be labelled AI-assessed, never presented as prevalence.

3. SHAREABLE READ-ONLY INSIGHT / REPORT LINKS (NO LOGIN)
   - What: public/presentation-mode links to a report or insight board for
     stakeholders who won't log in. Today sharing = Word/CSV export only.
   - Exemplars: Dovetail shareable insight boards & highlight reels.
   - Complexity: LOW-MEDIUM. High client demand.
   - Note: must respect ACL / transcript-access; link scope explicit.

4. PERMISSION-AWARE RE-RANKING (ON TOP OF EXISTING ACL)
   - What: rank accessible AND most-relevant content higher, beyond binary ACL
     filtering.
   - Exemplars: Glean retrieval-quality re-ranking.
   - Complexity: MEDIUM. Incremental retrieval improvement.

================================================================================
LARGER BETS (higher value, more build)
================================================================================

5. AI THEME-CLUSTERING & CROSS-WAVE SYNTHESIS
   - What: auto-group emergent themes across the whole longitudinal archive;
     narrative synthesis of patterns across many waves (not within one).
   - Exemplars: Dovetail "Magic Cluster" + cross-study synthesis.
   - Complexity: MEDIUM-HIGH. HIGH value — plays to our unique longitudinal
     asset. Realises the spec's "Early Warning Signals / emerging themes" (A10).

6. "DEEP RESEARCH"-STYLE STRUCTURED BRIEFINGS (EXTEND CREATE REPORT)
   - What: multi-retrieval synthesis into a longer structured briefing beyond
     templated first drafts.
   - Exemplars: NotebookLM Deep Research (15-25 sources -> structured briefing).
   - Complexity: MEDIUM-HIGH. Natural extension of Create Report.

7. AUDIO / VIDEO INGESTION WITH TRANSCRIPT-LINKED CLIPS + HIGHLIGHT REELS
   - What: store audio/video, timestamped transcripts, jump quote -> clip,
     assemble highlight reels.
   - Exemplars: Discuss.io (video editing + highlight reels), Conveo
     (multimodal: speech, tone, visual), Quantilope inColor (facial emotion).
   - Complexity: HIGH. The one category we fully lack.
   - Trigger: only worth it IF/WHEN source material becomes multimodal
     (currently Word/PPT/transcripts only).

================================================================================
OPTIONAL / WATCH
================================================================================

8. NOTEBOOKLM-STYLE AUDIO / VIDEO "OVERVIEWS" AS STAKEHOLDER DIGESTS
   - What: podcast-style two-host audio, or narrated-slide video, summary of a
     report/wave for stakeholders.
   - Exemplars: NotebookLM Audio Overviews / Video Overviews.
   - Complexity: MEDIUM-HIGH. Novel; lower priority than 1-6.

9. LIVE AI-MODERATED "QUAL AT QUANT SCALE" (ADJACENT, MONITOR ONLY)
   - What: live moderated discussions with real-time AI analytics surfacing
     themes/quotes/quantified sentiment and driving follow-up questions.
   - Exemplars: Remesh.
   - Relevance: adjacent primary-research capability, less relevant to a
     longitudinal archive, but shows where market expectations are heading.

RECOMMENDED ORDER
================================================================================

weeks; both map onto existing spec concepts (review gate, saved outputs).
Then:  #5 (cross-wave AI clustering) — the strategic bet on our unique asset.
Guardrail: every new AI feature inherits verification + caveats + traceability.
